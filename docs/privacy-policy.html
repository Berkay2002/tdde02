Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 11, 2025

TDDE02 ("the App") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we handle and why.

What We Collect

  • Photos you intentionally select and upload in the App.
  • Basic diagnostic data the platform provides (e.g., crash or performance logs) to keep the App reliable.

How We Use Data

  • Provide features that let you add, view, and manage your own photos.
  • Improve stability and performance using diagnostic data.

Sharing and Selling

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share personal data with third parties except service providers needed to run the App (e.g., cloud hosting) under confidentiality obligations.

Storage and Retention

  • Photos you upload are stored only to provide the requested features and retained until you delete them or remove your account data.
  • Diagnostic data is retained as needed for maintenance.

Permissions

READ_MEDIA_IMAGES (or its platform equivalent) is requested only when you choose to add/upload photos so you can pick from your gallery. Core usage remains available without granting it.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your Choices

  • You can decline photo access; photo-related features may be limited.
  • You may delete uploaded content from within the App where available.

Changes

We may update this policy and will adjust the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at https://github.com/Berkay2002/TDDE02.
